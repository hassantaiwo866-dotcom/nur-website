## 1. Purpose

The About section should explain why Nūr exists, what it believes about personal growth, and the kind of product it is trying to build.

It should feel human and grounded—not like a corporate manifesto, a founder biography written for prestige, or a promise that the product can solve every problem.

This is a brand-story specification. It does not establish that every part of the product vision is implemented.

## 2. Recommended positioning

**Core idea:** Personal growth needs more than restriction. It needs protection, practical choices, recovery and room to build a meaningful life.

**Short description:**

Nūr is being built to help people take greater ownership of their attention and everyday choices. It brings together protection, focus, recovery and personal growth—without shame, pressure or the expectation that progress must be perfect.

**Brand line:**

Build the person you want to become.

## 3. Proposed About page copy

### A different way to think about self-control

Many digital tools focus on stopping one behaviour. Nūr starts with a broader question:

**What would help you build the life you actually want to live?**

Sometimes that means creating stronger boundaries around what reaches you. Sometimes it means giving your attention to one important task. Sometimes it means recovering after a difficult moment, reflecting on what happened, or returning to something meaningful.

Nūr is being built around those connected needs.

### More than restriction

Protection can create space, but restriction alone is not the whole journey.

Nūr's direction brings together:

- **Protect** — create stronger boundaries around your attention.
- **Resist** — make a deliberate choice when an unwanted pattern appears.
- **Recover** — take a constructive next step without shame.
- **Rebuild** — strengthen routines and habits that matter to you.
- **Live** — make room for meaningful work, relationships, faith and everyday life.
- **Learn and adapt** — reflect on what helps and adjust your approach.

This is a guiding product framework, not a claim that every stage is already available in the current app.

### Progress without punishment

A setback does not erase a person's worth. A missed goal does not need to become a source of humiliation. And a tool designed to support self-control should not take control away from the person using it.

That is why Nūr is being shaped around a few clear principles:

- No shame-based messaging.
- No guilt as a motivational mechanism.
- No forced explanations after a difficult moment.
- No assumption that everyone has the same goals or beliefs.
- No unnecessary collection or sharing of personal information.
- No claim that software can make personal choices on someone's behalf.

### Built with privacy and choice in mind

Personal routines, struggles and reflections can be sensitive. Nūr's product direction is to minimise data collection, keep personal information under the user's control wherever practical, and make any sharing deliberate and understandable.

The actual storage, export, deletion and remote-service boundaries are described in the Privacy & Trust section. We will publish only controls and protections verified in the relevant release.

### For different people and different journeys

Nūr is intended for adults with different backgrounds, routines and motivations. Some may come for stronger digital boundaries. Others may want help focusing, rebuilding habits or reflecting on their choices.

Islamic Mode is optional. It is not a requirement for using Nūr, and Nūr is not a religious authority.

The aim is not to prescribe one ideal life. It is to offer practical tools that people can use in ways that fit their own values and circumstances.

### Still being built

Nūr is under active development. Some foundations exist in the app; other parts remain in design, testing or planned work. We will be clear about what is available, what is in testing and what has not yet been built.

We would rather earn trust through honest progress than make promises we cannot yet support.

## 4. Mission, vision and principles

### Mission

To help people protect their attention, practise self-control, recover without shame and build more intentional lives through practical, privacy-conscious tools.

### Long-term vision

A digital environment where technology supports people's intentions instead of constantly competing for their attention—and where personal growth is guided by agency, reflection and meaningful action.

This is an aspirational direction, not a claim about the current app's capabilities or measurable impact.

### Product principles

1. **Human agency:** The user remains in charge of their choices and settings.
2. **Privacy by design:** Minimise collection and make data handling understandable.
3. **No shame:** Treat difficulty as part of a human journey, not a character defect.
4. **Honest capability:** Explain limitations and distinguish released features from plans.
5. **Useful action:** Prefer practical, understandable tools over empty motivation.
6. **Inclusive by default:** Support different backgrounds and values; keep faith-specific experiences optional.
7. **Sustainable growth:** Build carefully, validate real usefulness and avoid manipulative engagement mechanics.

## 5. Founder and origin-story policy

The public About page does not yet contain a founder biography or a personal origin story.

Do not invent:
- A founding date or age.
- A dramatic personal struggle or recovery story.
- A claim that the founder built Nūr after experiencing a particular condition.
- Team size, credentials, professional qualifications or clinical expertise.
- Advisors, partners, investors or institutional endorsements.
- User counts, outcomes or impact statistics.

A founder note can be added later if the founder explicitly approves the exact biography, name/public identity, personal details and wording. Participation in the About page is optional; the product story should stand on its own.

Suggested future format, only after approval:

**A note from the founder**
A short first-person explanation of the problem that inspired Nūr, the principles guiding its development, and the commitment to build transparently. Keep it specific and factual. Do not frame the founder as a therapist, religious authority or expert unless independently qualified and relevant.

## 7. Design direction

- Follow the approved Nūr design system: forest-green foundation, refined serif headings and readable sans-serif body text.
- Use the concentric-light motif sparingly as a quiet visual thread.
- Prefer real product details and simple diagrams over generic stock photography.
- Use solid surfaces for long-form copy; avoid placing paragraphs on translucent glass.
- Keep the page readable on mid-range Android devices and narrow screens.
- Do not use dramatic before/after imagery, guilt-oriented visuals or depictions that label visitors as broken.
- Any animation must be subtle, optional and compatible with reduced-motion preferences.

## 8. Claims and evidence boundaries

- Describe the mission and principles as commitments guiding development, not independently proven outcomes.
- Describe the product loop as a framework; do not imply every stage is implemented.
- Do not claim Nūr improves mental health, treats addiction or guarantees recovery.
- Do not claim complete protection, perfect privacy or universal device support.
- Do not claim the product is fully open-source, independently audited, certified or legally compliant unless the relevant evidence exists.
- Link to Privacy & Trust and What Nūr Cannot Do for detailed boundaries.
- Re-check the current app release branch and implementation evidence before publishing any feature-specific statement.
